A pre-trained dynamic weighed priority-engine which takes in stimulated factors of hazard severity, infrastructure damage, population exposure, capacity stress and incident growth index to assign priority rankings for the zones of an affected remote area hit by a disaster or a calamity.Initially, built on opaque weights, but after repetitive training on actual flood -related datasets and artificially created datasets with relevant situation simulations, they are adjusted.
